
## Alarm and Xiaomi device guidance

The [official alarm guide](https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/services/alarms) says `setAlarmClock()` is the most user-visible exact event, is not adjusted by the system, and can wake low-power modes when necessary; it also warns that exact alarms consume resources. The announcement service therefore keeps the timed event in AlarmManager and uses a visible foreground notification. The [wake-lock best-practices guide](https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/background-tasks/awake/wakelock/best-practices) recommends a simple, bounded wake lock in a foreground service and reliable release; the alarm service now acquires a 10-minute partial wake lock and releases it on stop/destruction. Xiaomi's [official Background autostart FAQ](https://www.mi.com/global/support/faq/details/KA-507611/) describes Settings → Apps → Permissions → Background autostart on Xiaomi 14T Pro; because Xiaomi does not publish a stable cross-version deep-link action for that screen, the app offers an Android Settings link and version-specific instructions instead of claiming a guaranteed direct link. The app-info link opens Android's documented application details screen; users still must set Battery saver to “No restrictions” and optionally lock the app in Recents themselves.
